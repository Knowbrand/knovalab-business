So what exactly is this?

Two hosts. Three to five guests. One story that starts small and ends somewhere nobody planned.

Aadat Se Gareeb is not an interview show. Nobody sits in a chair answering serious questions while everyone else nods politely. It is a group conversation: someone tells a story, someone interrupts, someone asks a ridiculous question, and someone else remembers something even stranger.

You don't need to be a celebrity. You don't need followers. You need a story, and enough comfort with strangers to tell it out loud.

Three themes and one wildcard

These are the themes we keep coming back to. They are a starting point, not a fence. If your story fits none of them, there is a fourth option below.

Gareebi Ki Daastaan

Broke moments, strange ways of saving money, and the creative decisions people make when the bank balance isn't cooperating. We laugh with you, never at the struggle.

Think: the day you split one samosa three ways, or the budget plan that lasted until lunch.

Kaand Ho Gaya

Embarrassing moments, misunderstandings, and decisions that went spectacularly wrong. The kind of story your friends still bring up at every gathering.

Think: stories that sound completely made up but actually happened.

Bhoot Bhi Gareeb Tha

Creepy, unexplained, or paranormal experiences, told as personal stories. We don't claim anything is proven. We just want to hear what happened, especially if the ghost had a funny side.

Think: the strange coincidence, the weird sound, the horror story with an unexpected twist.

Something Completely Different

Got a story that doesn't fit? Good. We'd rather hear it than reject it on a technicality.

How it works

  1. Apply

    Send your details and a short teaser of your story. Just the trailer. Save the ending.

  2. We review

    The team reads every application and contacts the people we'd like to hear more from.

  3. Join the panel

    Selected participants get further details and join a remote session on Google Meet or Microsoft Teams.

  4. Let the conversation happen

    Tell your story, react to the others, and see where the group takes it.

Applying does not guarantee selection. We review applications before sending any invitations.

Who can join?

Almost anyone with a good story and a working camera.

  • You are at least 18 years old.
  • You are not required to be an influencer or a celebrity.
  • You have a story, experience, or perspective worth sharing.
  • You are comfortable taking part in a group conversation.
  • You have a suitable internet connection and a device that can join a video meeting.
  • You understand that final participation arrangements are shared after selection.
