What happens after applying?

  1. The team reads your application and story teaser.
  2. We contact the applicants we'd like to take further. Applying doesn't guarantee selection, and we may not be able to reply to everyone.
  3. Selected applicants receive session details for Google Meet or Microsoft Teams. Recording dates and times are still to be announced.
  4. Before anything is recorded, we ask for separate informed participation and recording consent.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to be famous?

No. Ordinary people with interesting stories are exactly who we're looking for.

Where will the show happen?

Online, through Google Meet or Microsoft Teams.

When will it happen?

Dates and times will be announced. Selected applicants hear from us directly.

Do I need to tell my entire story in the form?

No. Only a short teaser. Save the full story for the panel.

Will everyone who applies be selected?

No. Applications are reviewed before invitations are sent.

Can I participate without showing my face?

You can tell us your preference in the form. Final arrangements are discussed before participation.

What happens to my information?

It is used to review your application and to contact applicants about participation. More detail is in the privacy notice below.

Privacy notice

What we collect. The details you enter in the form: name, age, email, optional Instagram username, WhatsApp number and location, language, story category, camera preference, your story teaser, and the date you applied.

Why. To review applications and to contact applicants about taking part. The show team reads the applications.

Who processes it. Your submission is handled by a third-party form service that delivers it to the show team by email.

What it doesn't mean. Applying doesn't guarantee participation. It is also not consent to be recorded or published; we ask for separate consent before any recording.

Corrections and deletion. You can ask us to correct or delete your application using the request form below.
