About the show

Everyone has a story. Some should come with a warning.

Aadat Se Gareeb is an online comedy panel show about ordinary people, questionable decisions, and conversations that wander off in directions nobody planned. Here is the thinking behind it.

The idea

The show starts from one belief: ordinary people are often the most entertaining people in the room. Not because they rehearse, but because their lives keep handing them situations no writer would dare invent.

Everyone has a story. Some are hilarious. Some are embarrassing. Some are genuinely strange. And some make you wonder how the person survived long enough to tell them. We want to put those people together and let the conversation do its thing.

Our core line says it best: Gareebi sirf paison ki nahi hoti. Kabhi dimaag se bhi hote hain.

Why we exist

Not every entertaining person has a large following. Plenty of people can hold a room for twenty minutes with a story about a train ticket, a landlord, or a very bad idea, and never get a platform for it.

We want to make some space for them. Your story deserves an audience even if you've never been a public figure, and even if your follower count is embarrassing.

The format

Two regular hosts

They open the panel, start conversations, ask follow-up questions, keep things moving, and make sure guests are comfortable. They also bring their own humour into the mix.

Three to five guests

Each episode has a rotating group of roughly three to five guests. Different people, different personalities, different chemistry every time.

Group conversation

This is not a one-on-one interview. Everyone is meant to join in: react, interrupt (kindly), disagree, and add the story that is even worse.

Story themes

Three recurring themes guide the episodes: Gareebi Ki Daastaan, Kaand Ho Gaya, and Bhoot Bhi Gareeb Tha. Stories that fit none of them are welcome too.

Natural reactions

No scripted answers and no motivational speeches. The goal is a conversation that feels authentic, a little chaotic, and never mean-spirited.

What makes it different

The entertainment comes from the people and the way they bounce off each other, not from anyone's fame. A guest doesn't need a verified badge. They need a story and a willingness to share it with a group.

We are not trying to run a serious discussion or an educational session. We are trying to make something people actually enjoy watching.

The experience

Sessions happen online, on Google Meet or Microsoft Teams, so you can take part from wherever you are with a suitable internet connection and a device that can join a video meeting.

If you are selected, you will receive further instructions before anything is recorded. Recording dates and times are still to be announced, and we will share them with selected applicants directly.

Applying is not consent to being recorded or published. Separate informed consent is obtained before any recording.

The spirit of the show

  • Humour. The funnier the better, as long as the joke isn't at someone's expense.
  • Authenticity. Real experiences, told your way.
  • Respect. Money trouble is relatable, not a punchline. Nobody gets harassed or humiliated for entertainment.
  • Participation. A panel works when everyone contributes.
  • Privacy. Don't share other people's private information without their permission.
