About Konnichiwa Pune

A free festival that brings students, professionals and families closer to Japan, organised by the Indo-Japan Business Council (IJBC) and the Consulate General of Japan in Mumbai.

About IJBC

The Indo-Japan Business Council is a non-profit industry association that promotes collaboration between India and Japan in business, trade, education and culture.

Founded in 2011, IJBC works with businesses, academic institutions, professionals and government bodies to strengthen bilateral ties and create long-term partnerships. It is the apex bilateral chamber for India–Japan engagement and has a pan-India presence.

Vision and mission

To foster people-to-people connections and deepen India–Japan relations by:

  1. Encouraging economic cooperation
  2. Promoting India as an attractive destination for Japanese investment
  3. Supporting shared growth through collaboration
  1. Serve as a reliable platform for information, consulting and business advice
  2. Promote industries and trade opportunities between India and Japan
  3. Support networking and knowledge-sharing across sectors
  4. Recognise outstanding contributions to Indo-Japan social and economic development

Key activities

IJBC promotes India–Japan partnerships across business, education and culture.

Business delegations

Structured visits to explore investment and collaboration opportunities.

Academic exchange

University partnerships, joint research and student mobility.

Events and conferences

Bilateral forums for trade, policy dialogue and knowledge sharing.

SME support

Help with market entry, networking and bilateral growth.

Bilingual expert directory

Professionals fluent in Japanese and Indian languages, connected with organisations.

Infrastructure collaboration

Infrastructure development aligned with Japanese industry needs in India.

Technology and insights

Updates on emerging technologies, regulation and sector trends.

Recognition and awards

Honouring people and organisations for their role in India–Japan relations.
