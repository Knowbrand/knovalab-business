What is Konnichiwa Pune?

Konnichiwa Pune brings Japanese culture closer to Pune through traditional Japanese arts, contemporary culture, food, performances and hands-on experiences.

Students, professionals and families come together to learn about Japan. Stay for the performances, try something new or explore at your own pace.

Festival highlights

  • Anime and Cosplay. Experience Japanese pop culture.
  • All-in-one celebration. Business, culture, education.
  • Delectable cuisines. Authentic flavours of Japan.
  • Diverse cultural programmes. Mesmerising art displays.
  • Artistic fusion. Japanese and Indian artists showcase talents.
  • Wear a Japanese kimono. Experience the traditional attire.
  • Bilingual experts. A knowledge exchange.
  • Craftsmanship. Engaging workshops and demos.
  • Japanese souvenirs. Take home a piece of Japan.
  • Lasting connections. Friendship beyond borders.
